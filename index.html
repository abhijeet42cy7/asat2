Algorithms used for Eucalyptus Identification

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)
(B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04)

High canopy vigor (0.65 - 0.88) typical in dense young eucalyptus stands.

NDRE (Normalized Difference Red Edge)
(B08 - B05) / (B08 + B05)

Penetrates dense canopy without NDVI saturation, detecting eucalyptus chlorophyll levels.

MSI (Moisture Stress Index / SWIR)
B11 / B08

Distinguishes broadleaf eucalyptus from conifers due to leaf water absorption and pendulous leaf angle.
